Key Features

Explore the core features and capabilities of LightMaster Pro.

Improve Your Renders

Turn basic default lighting into a more expressive and cinematic look. Light Master Pro helps you add stronger mood and more artistic light variation to make your renders feel more unique and visually polished.

Advanced Placement

Place and aim lights directly in the viewport with interactive controls and fast iteration.

400+ Lighting Assets

Choose from more than 400 ready-made assets across multiple categories, including Abstract Light, Caustic, Colored, Flashlight, Light Reflections, Rays, Shadows, Simple Gobos, and Water.

Animation Feature

Enable dynamic asset movement with Flow Maps to add motion and visual interest, while keeping the system lightweight and avoiding unnecessary memory usage.
